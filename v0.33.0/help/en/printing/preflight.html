    Preflight

    Preflight checks a drawing for what tends to go wrong in print, before you print it or send it to a print shop. Choose File > Preflight. The Print dialog box also runs the checks and shows how many issues it found.

    The checks#

    CheckFinds
    Empty text objecttext with nothing in it
    Text does not fit its frameparagraph text with hidden lines
    Font not installedtext in a font that is not installed
    Very small texttext smaller than 5 points as printed
    Small text in rich blacksmall black text made of several inks
    RGB color in a CMYK drawingscreen colors in a drawing meant for print
    Object off the pageobjects outside the page and its bleed
    Low-resolution bitmapbitmaps below 200 dpi as placed
    Very high-resolution bitmapbitmaps above 1200 dpi
    Very thin outlineoutlines thinner than 0.1 mm
    White set to overprintwhite that would vanish when printed
    Empty pagepages with no objects
    Objects that will not printobjects on hidden or non-printing layers

    Warnings are likely problems; notes are worth a look.

    Using the list#

    The top of the box shows the outcome. Each kind of issue has a heading with its count; click it to open or close it. Click an issue to go to its page and select its object. Under the list, the box says what to do about the kind you picked.

    • Check again runs the checks again after you fix things.
    • Choose checks turns checks on and off and sets their limits: the bitmap resolutions, the smallest text sizes and the thinnest outline. Defaults puts them back.
