    Time tracker

    The time tracker records the time you spend on a drawing, task by task, and keeps the records in the drawing (in .tdraw files). Time counts only while you work: after a few idle minutes the clock waits, and your next action starts it again.

    The Time Tracker toolbar#

    Window > Toolbars > Time Tracker shows a bar with:

    • the list of tasks, to choose the active task, or add a new one;
    • the time of the active task;
    • the record and pause button;
    • a light: red while counting, amber while idle, gray when paused or without a task;
    • the total of every task;
    • a button that opens the dialog box.

    Starting the clock on a drawing without tasks adds "Task 1".

    The Time Tracker dialog box#

    Tools > Time Tracker lists the tasks with their time, start and finish, and the total.

    • New task, Delete, Make active and Reset time act on the selected task. Double-click a task to make it active.
    • Record and Pause start and stop the clock.
    • Edit the selected task's name, time (such as 1:30:00, 1:30 or 1.5 hours), start and finish (such as 2026-10-19 14:30).
    • Stop counting after N minutes without input sets the idle time (5 minutes at first).
    • Export CSV and Export text save the records, for a spreadsheet or a report.

    Recording time is not an edit of the drawing: it makes no undo step. The records are saved with the drawing's next save.
