    Pomoc programu TraceDraw/Strony i układ/Snapping

    Ta strona nie jest jeszcze przetłumaczona i jest wyświetlana po angielsku.

    Snapping

    Snapping pulls the pointer, and the objects you move, onto exact points: nodes, the middles of segments, the centers of shapes, guidelines, the grid or the page. It makes it easy to join lines and line objects up without zooming in.

    Turning snapping on and off#

    • View > Snap To, or the Snap To list on the standard toolbar, turns snapping to Pixels, Document Grid (Ctrl+Y), Baseline Grid, Guidelines, Objects (Alt+Z) and Page on and off. At first objects, guidelines and the page are on. The baseline grid works on its own too: with only it on, nodes and objects snap to its lines.
    • Snap Off (Alt+Q) turns all snapping off and on again.

    What objects snap to#

    When snapping to objects is on, these points attract the pointer within the snapping radius (10 screen pixels at first). Each shows its mark and, with screen tips on, its name.

    ModePoints
    Nodethe nodes of outlines, the corners of rectangles, the points of polygons
    Intersectionwhere two outlines cross
    Midpointhalfway along each segment
    Quadrantthe top, bottom, left and right of ellipses
    Tangentwhere a line you draw touches an ellipse or curve
    Perpendicularwhere a line you draw meets a segment at a right angle
    Edgethe nearest point of an outline
    Centerthe center of shapes, bitmaps and tables
    Text baselinethe baseline of a line of text

    Points win over guidelines, guidelines over the grids, and edges come last. The modes, the radius and the marks are set on the Snapping page of the options; see TraceDraw options.

    Snapping while moving#

    When you start moving a selection with the Pick tool on one of its points (a node, a midpoint or the center), that point is carried onto the other object's point or edge. Otherwise the edges and center of the selection's box snap to guidelines, grids, the page and other objects. The handles snap while you resize.

    Dynamic and alignment guides#

    • View > Dynamic Guides (Alt+Shift+D) shows temporary guides at 0, 45, 90 and 135 degrees through the points you pass over, so you can draw along them.
    • Alignment Guides (Alt+Shift+A) shows guides when the object you move lines up with others.
