    TraceCut

    Tools > TraceCut opens TraceCut, the tools for cutting plotters. It is also on the standard toolbar's Launch button. Select the objects to work on before opening it.

    The tools are listed on the left by group. On the right are the chosen tool's settings, a preview of the selection and what the tool finds, and the buttons:

    • Apply shows the result in the preview without changing the drawing;
    • OK applies it and closes the window; anything it changes is undone in one step;
    • Cancel closes the window without a change.

    To hide TraceCut, clear Show TraceCut in Launch and the Tools menu in Options, General.

    Presets#

    Each tool keeps its settings under names you give. Pick a preset from the list to load it. The gear button shows the row to Save the settings under a name, Delete a preset, or go back to the Defaults.

    Plotter Output#

    Cuts the selection on a cutting plotter. Choose the machine (the commands your cutter understands), the knife offset of your blade (often 0.25 mm), the overcut that closes each outline, the passes for thick material, and where the job goes:

    • File saves a .plt file;
    • Port sends it to a serial or USB-serial port, such as /dev/ttyUSB0 or COM3 (set the port's speed to the cutter's first, with your system's tools);
    • Network sends it to the cutter's address, port 9100 unless you give another.

    Plan the cut order cuts the nearest outline next and the inner outlines first. Test cut cuts a small square with a triangle inside, to check the knife before the real job. Save your cutter's settings as a preset.

    Weeding#

    Adds weeding lines from each object to its neighbours, or a box around each object, and a weeding frame, so the waste peels off in small pieces.

    Bridges#

    Leaves short uncut stretches in closed outlines, so the parts stay in the sheet until you break them out.

    Cut Order#

    Reorders the objects and their outlines for the shortest travel of the knife, inner outlines first, and sets where each outline starts and which way it runs.

    CutContour#

    Gives the selected objects an outline in the spot color CutContour, the name print and cut workflows look for, and can remove their fill. The name is kept when you export to PDF, EPS or SVG.

    Registration Marks#

    Adds the marks a cutter's sensor reads for print and cut, around the selection or inside the page: squares, circles or L-shaped corners, with more along long sides.

    Find Duplicates#

    Objects lying exactly on top of another one would be cut twice. Set the tolerance, how far apart two outlines may be and still be the same, and choose whether OK deletes the repeats or selects them. The lowest object of each set stays.

    Find Crossings#

    Outlines that cross themselves cut badly, and objects that cross each other usually need a weld first. TraceCut finds the crossings, shows them as red rings in the preview, and OK selects the objects that have them. Tick Also between objects to find crossings between different objects too.

    Nesting#

    Shape Nesting places the selected objects on sheets or a roll by their true outlines, turning them and fitting small ones into holes, to waste as little material as possible. Rectangle Nesting does the same by their boxes, faster. Set the sheet or roll size, the margin and the spacing between objects; OK moves the objects onto the material and can draw the sheets.

    Measuring#

    Perimeter and Area gives the outline length and the area of the selection, holes taken away. Cutting Time works out how long the cutter takes, from its speeds. Cost prices the job: the material by area or by the length of roll, cutting, weeding, pieces and machine time. These tools change nothing in the drawing.
