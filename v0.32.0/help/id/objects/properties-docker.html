    The Properties panel

    The Properties panel (Alt+Enter) shows and changes the properties of the selected objects: their outline, fill, transparency and more, depending on what is selected. It is open at first, beside Hints and Objects. Open it again with Object > Properties.

    With objects selected#

    The top of the panel names the selected object (and how many more are selected) and gives its position and size. Under it, a row of buttons opens and closes each section the object has:

    • QR code, for a QR code: its content and look; see Barcodes and QR codes.
    • Outline: color, width, paint, caps, corners, dashes, arrowheads and more; see Setting outlines.
    • Fill: the kind of fill and its settings; see Fills. F11 opens this section.
    • Transparency: how much of what lies below shows through; see Transparency.
    • Rectangle, for rectangles: the corner settings; see Corners.
    • FX: the bitmap effects applied to the object; see Bitmap effects.
    • Character, for text: the font, size, Bold and Italic, and a button to Edit with the Text tool (F8).
    • Summary: a summary of the object.

    The sections you closed stay closed the next time. A change applies to every selected object and is one undo step.

    With nothing selected#

    With nothing selected, the panel says No objects selected and shows the Default properties for new objects: the fill and outline that new shapes and lines get. Change them here to draw with other colors from the start. See Object styles and defaults.
