    Vector and picture mosaics

    A mosaic rebuilds the selected objects, or a picture, out of many small tiles. A vector mosaic uses colored shapes; a picture mosaic uses photos from a folder of your own.

    Vector Mosaic#

    Open the Vector Mosaic panel with Effects > Vector Mosaic.

    1. Select the objects or bitmaps to turn into a mosaic.
    2. Set the options:
      • Density: tiles per inch (10 at first); Scale: the size of each tile within its cell (1 at first); Screen angle: how the rows are turned (30 degrees at first).
      • Keep original: off deletes the selection once the mosaic is made.
      • Limit colors: reduce the tiles to at most that many colors (16 at first).
      • Method: Uniform (every tile full size, transparency flattened on white), Size modulation 1 (the tile size follows the opacity, at first) or Size modulation 2 (darker areas give bigger tiles).
      • Merge adjacent: combine up to that many neighbouring tiles of one color; Weld adjacent overlap: one curve per color instead of one object per tile.
      • Shape: circle (at first), square or custom. For a custom tile, select a closed curve and click Select.
    3. Click Apply.

    The mosaic is one group placed on top of the selection, and is selected.

    Picture Mosaic#

    Open the Picture Mosaic panel with Effects > Picture Mosaic.

    1. Select the objects or bitmaps to rebuild.
    2. Under Library, click Browse and choose a folder of pictures (PNG, JPEG, BMP, GIF, WebP or TIFF; up to 1000). The panel shows how many it holds.
    3. Set the options:
      • Keep original (on at first).
      • Columns: tiles across (30 at first); the rows follow from the shape of the selection. Blending: how much of the selection shows over the tiles (20% at first).
      • Allow duplicates (on), with the tile spacing: the fewest tiles between two uses of one picture.
      • Composition: single, stack or array; Edges: stretch the last row or remove it; Output priority and the resolution: how large the mosaic is in pixels. The panel shows the resulting size.
    4. Click Apply.
    Note: Picture libraries need the desktop version; the browser version cannot read folders.
