    Aligning and distributing objects

    Aligning lines objects up by their edges or centers; distributing spaces them evenly. Use the Align and Distribute panel (Ctrl+Shift+A), the commands of Object > Align and Distribute, or the keys below.

    Aligning#

    1. Select the objects. Select the one the others should line up with last.
    2. In the Align part of the panel, choose Align to:
      • Active object (at first): the object selected last; with one object selected, the page;
      • Page edge: the edges of the page;
      • Page center: the center of the page;
      • Grid: each object to the nearest line of the document grid.
    3. Click one of the six buttons: left edges, centers on a vertical line, right edges, top edges, centers on a horizontal line, or bottom edges.

    Use outline: align the outer edges of the outlines: with this on, the outer edges of the outlines meet, instead of the paths themselves.

    The keys always align to the object selected last:

    KeyCommand
    LAlign Left
    RAlign Right
    TAlign Top
    BAlign Bottom
    EAlign Centers Horizontally
    CAlign Centers Vertically
    PCenter to Page

    Distributing#

    1. Select three or more objects (two or more to distribute across the page).
    2. In the Distribute part, choose Distribute to:
      • Selection extent (at first): the first and last objects stay where they are, and the others move between them;
      • Page extent: the objects spread across the whole page, the first touching one side and the last the other.
    3. Click one of the eight buttons: left edges, centers, gaps or right edges across the page, or top edges, centers, gaps or bottom edges down it. Spreading the gaps gives equal space between the objects whatever their sizes.

    With too few objects the buttons are pale and the panel says how many are needed. Each align or distribute is one undo step.

    To place copies at even distances in one step, see Step and repeat.
