    Distortion

    The Distort tool bends an object's outline in three ways: push and pull (spikes or petals), zipper (zigzag teeth) and twister (a swirl). It is a live effect: you can change, move or remove the distortion at any time, and the object keeps its own outline.

    To distort an object#

    1. Choose the Distort tool from the Effects flyout.
    2. Choose the kind on the property bar.
    3. Drag on the object. The distortion is centered where you start.
      • Push and pull: drag right to push the nodes out (spiky shapes with hollow sides), left to pull them in (rounded petals).
      • Zipper: the further you drag, the taller the teeth.
      • Twister: drag around the starting point; full turns count.

    The center of a selected distortion shows as a diamond: drag it to move the center. Dragging again replaces the distortion; an object has at most one. Groups and bitmaps are not distorted.

    Distortion settings#

    The property bar shows and changes the selected distortion:

    • the kind: Push and pull, Zipper or Twister (choosing another kind changes a selected distortion);
    • for push and pull, the amplitude (-200 to 200, 20 at first);
    • for zipper, the amplitude (0 to 100 mm, 2 mm at first) and the frequency (teeth per segment, 6 at first), with Random (uneven teeth), Smooth (rounded teeth) and Local (teeth shrink away from the center);
    • for twister, clockwise or counterclockwise, the number of whole rotations and the extra degrees.

    A value changed on the property bar shows on the selected object at once, also when it has no distortion yet. The zipper draws one more line than its frequency, and its amplitude, frequency and angle have handles on the page to drag. The twister spirals smoothly to the ends of a shape.

    Object > Clear Effect removes the distortion.
