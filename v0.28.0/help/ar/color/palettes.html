    تعليمات TraceDraw/الألوان والتعبئة والشفافية/Color palettes

    لم تُترجم هذه الصفحة بعد وتُعرض بالإنجليزية.

    Color palettes

    The color palette along the bottom of the window is the quickest way to color objects. Below it, the document palette collects the colors your drawing uses. You can open more palettes, make your own and edit them.

    Using the palette#

    ActionResult
    Click a colorfills the selection (with nothing selected, sets the fill of new objects)
    Right-click a colorcolors the outline of the selection
    Ctrl+click a colormixes a tenth of it into the selection's fill
    Click and hold a colorshows lighter, darker, duller and brighter shades of it to choose from
    Drag a color onto an objectfills the object, or colors its outline when dropped on the edge
    Click the crossed-out wellremoves the fill (right-click: the outline)

    The arrows at the ends scroll the palette, and the button at the right end shows every color at once. Hover a color to see its name and values. The palette menu button at the left end opens the palette menu.

    The palette can be moved to another edge of the window, or made to float: drag it by its dotted grip. With two or three rows, the window showing every color opens clear of all the rows.

    Every palette ticked in Window > Color Palettes or in the Palette Manager shows in a strip of its own next to the default palette, with Close Palette in its menu.

    The document palette#

    Every color you apply to an object joins the document palette, which is saved with the drawing. Its eyedropper adds the color under the pointer, and its menu has Automatically update (to stop adding colors by themselves), Add from selection, Add from document, Delete color and Reset palette (keeps only the colors the drawing still uses). You can also drag colors or objects onto it. The color used last has a white frame: a color taken with the eyedropper that the palette already has gets the frame, so you see where it is, and applying a color of the palette moves the frame to it. To show or hide it, see Customization options.

    The Palettes panel#

    The Palettes panel lists the palettes you have, with the number of colors in each. Open it with Window > Color Palettes > Palette Manager.

    • Tick a palette to show it under the drawing window.
    • Edit opens it in the palette editor.
    • Open Palette opens a palette file (.tdpal, .gpl, .ase or .aco).
    • Create Palette from Document and Create Palette from Selection make a palette of the colors used in the drawing or in the selection.
    • The document palette's colors show at the bottom; click one to fill the selection, right-click to color the outline.

    Editing a palette#

    Window > Color Palettes > Palette Editor opens the palette editor.

    1. Choose the Palette to edit, or click New to start one.
    2. Type its Name.
    3. Click a color to select it; Add color adds the current color and Remove color removes the selected one.
    4. Click Save palette to save it as a file.
    5. Click Close.

    Built-in palettes cannot be edited; make a new palette, or edit a copy.
