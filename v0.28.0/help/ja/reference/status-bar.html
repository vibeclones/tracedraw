    The status bar

    The status bar runs along the bottom of the window (right-click it to move it to the top). From the left:

    1. The settings button chooses what the first field shows: tool hints (at first), object details, cursor coordinates or the document's color settings.
    2. The first field: a hint for the active tool, the size and center of the selection, the pointer's position, or the color profiles. While you move, scale, rotate or skew objects, it shows the distance, scale or angle.
    3. The object information: what is selected and on which layer, such as "Rectangle on Layer 1" or "2 Objects Selected on Layer 1"; with the Shape tool, the number of nodes of a curve.
    4. The fill of the selection: a swatch and its description. Double-click it to change the fill.
    5. The outline of the selection: a swatch, the color and the width. Double-click it to change the outline.
    6. The proof colors button: shows colors as they will print; see Color management. Beside it, a button switches between the light and dark themes.

    With nothing selected, the fill and outline parts show those of new objects. To show or hide the status bar, use Window > Toolbars > Status Bar.
