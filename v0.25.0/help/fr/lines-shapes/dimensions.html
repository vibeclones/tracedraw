    Dimension lines and callouts

    Dimension lines show the size of things in a technical drawing: a length, a horizontal or vertical distance, an angle or the length of a segment. Callouts point at something with a leader line and a label. Both stay live: when you move, scale or turn them, they are measured and drawn again.

    Placing a dimension#

    Every dimension is placed in clicks, and the property bar says what to click next. While the last point is wanted, the dimension follows the pointer. Esc starts again. Points snap as drawing does, so snapping to nodes or objects gives exact measures; see Snapping. The new dimension is selected.

    Parallel#

    The Parallel Dimension tool measures the straight distance between two points, at any angle:

    1. Drag from the first point to the second, or click both.
    2. Move the pointer and click where the dimension line goes.

    Horizontal or Vertical#

    The Horizontal or Vertical Dimension tool measures only the horizontal or the vertical distance between two points, whichever is larger. Place it as a parallel dimension.

    Angular#

    The Angular Dimension tool measures an angle:

    1. Click the vertex of the angle.
    2. Click a point on the first side, then a point on the second side.
    3. Click where the arc goes.

    The angle is measured counterclockwise from the first side.

    Segment#

    The Segment Dimension tool measures a segment of an object: click the segment to take its two ends, then click where the dimension line goes.

    Dimension style#

    The property bar shows these settings with the dimension tools and when the selection is all dimensions. Changes apply to the selected dimensions and to the next ones you make.

    • Precision: 0 to 6 decimals (2 at first).
    • Units: the drawing units (at first) or any other unit. Lengths follow the drawing scale, so at 1:100 a 10 mm line reads 1000 mm; see Document options.
    • Show units: the unit after the number (the degree sign for angles).
    • Prefix and Suffix: text before and after the number.
    • Text: above the line (at first), in the line, or below the line.
    • Label size: 1 to 500 points (10 at first).

    The label always reads left to right and stays upright.

    Callout#

    The 3-Point Callout tool draws a label with a leader line in two segments.

    1. Drag from the tip (what the callout points at) to the elbow, or click both.
    2. Click where the text goes. The text goes on that side of the elbow.
    3. Type the text. The sample text is selected, so typing replaces it.

    The leader always ends at the text: when you type more, change the size or move the text on its own (Ctrl+click it), the leader follows. Move the tip or the elbow with the Shape tool.

    The property bar, shown with the tool and when the selection is all callouts, sets:

    • the outline width of the leader and shape (a hairline at first);
    • the line style;
    • the head at the tip (an arrow at first, or none);
    • the callout shape around the text: none (at first), underline, rectangle, rounded rectangle or ellipse;
    • the gap between the text and the leader or shape (1 mm at first).
