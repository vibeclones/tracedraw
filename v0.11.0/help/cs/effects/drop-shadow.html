    Drop shadows

    A drop shadow is a soft shadow beneath an object, as if it floated above the page. It is a copy of the object's silhouette in the shadow color, offset and blurred.

    To add a drop shadow#

    1. Choose the Drop Shadow tool from the Effects flyout.
    2. Drag from an object to where the shadow should fall.

    Settings#

    On the property bar:

    • the offset of the shadow (2 mm right and 2 mm down at first);
    • its opacity (50% at first);
    • its feathering, how soft its edge is (15% at first);
    • its color (black at first).

    Object > Clear Effect removes the shadow. To copy the shadow of another object, use Object > Copy Effect > Drop Shadow From.
