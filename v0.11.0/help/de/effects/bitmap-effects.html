    Bitmap effects

    Bitmap effects change pixels: blur, sharpen, adjust colors, add textures and artistic strokes. They work on bitmaps and also on vector objects, which are then drawn as the picture the effects make while keeping their own shape, fill and outline.

    Effects are kept apart from the original pixels: you can edit, hide, reorder or remove any of them later, and removing the last one gives the original back.

    To apply a bitmap effect#

    1. Select one or more objects.
    2. Choose a group in the Effects menu and an effect.
    3. Effects without settings apply at once. The others open a dialog box with before and after previews of the first selected object: set the values and click OK. Reset puts every setting back to its default.

    The change to the whole selection is one undo step. With a bitmap selected, Ctrl+B opens Brightness/Contrast/Intensity, Ctrl+Shift+B Color Balance and Ctrl+Shift+U Hue/Saturation/Lightness.

    Special dialogs#

    • Image Adjustments opens a larger dialog box: a large preview you can zoom, pan and rotate, shown alone, side by side or split; Auto adjust and pickers for the white and black points; sliders for temperature, tint, saturation, brightness, contrast, highlights, shadows and midtones, with a histogram; undo and redo inside the dialog; and snapshots of settings to compare.
    • Contrast Enhancement shows the histogram with markers to drag for the input and output levels, eyedroppers for black and white, and Auto adjust.
    • Tone Curve edits a curve per channel (master, red, green, blue) in four styles (curve, straight, freehand, gamma), over the histogram; curves can be saved and loaded.
    • Colors of the picture, such as in Replace Colors, have an eyedropper: click a preview to take the color under the pointer.

    Editing the effects of an object#

    The FX section of the Properties panel lists the effects of the selected object, first applied at the top. Each row has a check box to show or hide the effect, Edit (opens its dialog box), Move up, Move down and Delete. Add effect opens the effect groups.

    Effects > Flatten Effects makes the effects permanent: a bitmap keeps what it shows, and a vector object becomes a bitmap.

    The Effect Picker#

    Effects > Effect Picker opens a window that stays open while you work, with every effect as a small picture. Type in the search field to find effects, or choose a group or Recent from the list. With something selected, click a tile to apply the effect. You can also apply an effect as a lens.

    Effects that spread past the edges#

    Blurs and similar effects can reach past the edges of a bitmap. With Bitmaps > Inflate Bitmap > Auto Inflate Bitmap on (as at first), the bitmap grows to make room for them. See Resampling and straightening.

    To save a bitmap with its effects as a PNG file, right-click it and choose Save Bitmap As.
