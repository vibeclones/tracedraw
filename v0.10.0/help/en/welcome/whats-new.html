    What's new

    Each version of TraceDraw adds features and fixes. This page explains how to find out what changed and lists the highlights of the latest versions.

    Seeing what is new in the program#

    • Choose Help > What's New? to open the What's New page of the Welcome Screen, with the news of this version.
    • Turn on Help > Highlight What's New to mark what is new in this version in the menus: a small dot appears at the end of each new command, beside the arrow of a submenu that leads to one, and over the title of a menu that holds one. The marks go away when you turn the command off, and the command is off again the next time TraceDraw starts.
    • The complete list of changes of every version is in the project's change log.

    Highlights of recent versions#

    • Help in your language. Every ? button and F1 open the page of this Help about the dialog box, panel or tool in front of you, in the language TraceDraw is set to.
    • Document properties. File > Document Properties keeps the language, title, subject, author, copyright, tags, notes and rating of a drawing, over a full report of what the drawing holds. See Document properties.
    • Color management. Tools > Color Management sets the profiles of the drawing and the defaults for new ones, with policies for files that open or are imported. See Color management.
    • Barcodes and QR codes. A three-step wizard places barcodes, and QR codes are live objects you edit in the Properties panel. See Barcodes and QR codes.
    • Effects. The Effects menu groups the effect panels; blends can follow a path with fixed spacing and turn their colors clockwise or counterclockwise. See Blends.
    • Text on a path has its own settings for orientation, placement, distance and offset. See Adding and editing text.
    • Font Manager organizes installed fonts, folders, favorites and collections. See Fonts and glyphs.
