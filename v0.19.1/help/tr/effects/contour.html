    Contours

    A contour draws evenly spaced copies of an object's outline inside it, outside it, or to its center, like the lines of a map. It is a live effect: change the object and the contour follows.

    To add a contour#

    With the tool:

    1. Choose the Contour tool from the Effects flyout.
    2. Drag from an object: away from its middle for an outside contour, towards it for an inside one. The further you drag, the larger the spacing.

    With the panel:

    1. Select an object.
    2. Open the Contour panel with Effects > Contour (Ctrl+F9).
    3. Set the options and click Apply.

    Groups and bitmaps cannot take a contour.

    Contour settings#

    The property bar and the panel show the settings of the selected contour and change it at once.

    • To center, Inside or Outside (at first).
    • Steps: 3 at first, up to 999. To center makes as many as fit.
    • Offset: the distance between steps (2 mm at first).
    • Corners: mitered (sharp, at first), round or beveled.
    • Color direction: direct, clockwise or counterclockwise around the color wheel, from the object's colors to the last step's.
    • The fill and outline colors of the last step (white and black at first). An object without a fill gives unfilled steps.
    • Object acceleration and color acceleration (-100 to 100%) crowd the steps or the colors towards the object or towards the last step.

    Outside steps are drawn behind the object, inside steps in front of it.

    Clear contour removes the contour; Break apart turns the steps into objects, grouped beside the object. Object > Break Contour Group Apart (Ctrl+K, also on the right-click menu) does the same.
