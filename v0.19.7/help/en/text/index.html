    Text

    TraceDraw has two kinds of text. Artistic text is a line, or a few lines, placed at a point: titles, labels, logos. Paragraph text flows inside a frame and wraps at its edges: body copy, columns, lists. Both can be formatted character by character, fitted to paths and checked for spelling.

    These pages explain how to add and format text, lay out paragraphs, choose fonts and special characters, and use the writing tools.
