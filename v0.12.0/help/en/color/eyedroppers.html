    Copying colors and attributes with the eyedroppers

    The eyedropper tools take a color, or a whole set of attributes, from one object and give it to others. Both work in two steps, shown on the property bar: select (take a sample) and apply (paint with it). After a sample, the tool switches to apply; click the select button to sample again. Esc drops the sample and goes back to the Pick tool.

    The pointer is a pipette while the tool samples and a paint bucket while it applies. Pictures give their color even inside a group or a frame.

    Color Eyedropper#

    1. Choose the Color Eyedropper tool.
    2. Click an object to take its color: the fill color of a uniform fill, the first color of a gradient, or the outline color. On a bitmap, it takes the color of the pixels under the pointer, averaged over the sample size set on the property bar (1 by 1, 2 by 2 or 5 by 5 pixels). Shift+click takes the outline color first.
    3. Click objects to fill them with that color; Shift+click to color their outline (an object without an outline gets a thin one). Clicking inside an empty closed shape fills it.

    The property bar shows the sampled color.

    Attributes Eyedropper#

    The Attributes Eyedropper tool copies several properties at once from one object to others.

    1. Choose the Attributes Eyedropper tool.
    2. On the property bar, choose what to copy:
      • Properties: outline, fill and text (the font, size and style; the text itself is kept). All on at first.
      • Transformations: size, rotation and position. Off at first.
      • Effects: transparency, drop shadow and live effects. Off at first.
    3. Click the object to copy from.
    4. Click each object to copy to. Shift+click samples another object instead.

    Each click is one undo step.
