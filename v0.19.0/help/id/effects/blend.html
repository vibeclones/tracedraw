    Blends

    A blend draws a series of intermediate steps between two objects, changing shape, size and color from one to the other. Use it for smooth shading, repeated patterns or a shape that turns into another.

    To blend two objects#

    With the tool:

    1. Choose the Blend tool from the Effects flyout.
    2. Drag from the start object to the end object.

    With the panel:

    1. Select two objects.
    2. Open the Blend panel with Effects > Blend (Ctrl+F8).
    3. Set the options and click Apply.

    Both objects stay objects of the drawing: move, resize or recolor either one and the steps follow. Deleting the end object removes the blend.

    Blend settings#

    With a blend selected, every change on the property bar or in the panel applies at once.

    • Blend steps: the number of steps (5 at first, up to 999).
    • Spacing, for a blend on a path: the distance between steps; the number of steps follows from the length of the path.
    • Rotation: turns the steps progressively; Loop turns them about the middle between the two objects instead.
    • Acceleration: the object slider makes the steps crowd towards one end; the color slider does the same for the colors. Apply to sizing makes the object acceleration change the sizes too, and Link acceleration keeps the two sliders together. Reset puts both back to even.
    • Color Blend: Direct (straight from one color to the other), Clockwise or Counterclockwise (around the color wheel).

    The tool's property bar has the steps, rotation and the two accelerations.

    Blending along a path#

    1. Select the blend.
    2. Click New path in the Blend panel and click a curve.

    The steps follow the curve. Rotate all objects turns each step along the curve, and Blend along full path places the first and last steps at the ends of the curve.

    Removing or splitting a blend#

    Clear blend removes the steps and leaves both objects. Break apart turns the steps into objects of their own, grouped. Object > Break Blend Group Apart (Ctrl+K, also on the right-click menu) does the same.
