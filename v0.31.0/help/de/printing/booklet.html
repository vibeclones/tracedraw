    Printing booklets

    A booklet prints two pages on each side of a sheet of paper, placed so that the folded sheets read in order: a menu, a program, a small catalog or a zine. TraceDraw pairs the pages for you; you choose how the booklet is bound.

    Printing a booklet#

    1. Put the pages of the drawing in reading order, the cover first.
    2. Choose File > Print (Ctrl+P) or Print Preview.
    3. On the Layout tab, choose Booklet as the Imposition.
    4. Under Binding, click one of the two pictures (see below).
    5. Set the binding's fields: Sheets per signature for a perfect-bound booklet, Creep for a saddle-stitched one, and the Gap for either.
    6. Check the sketch and the counts under it, then print on both sides of the paper.

    To make a booklet of only some pages, list them in the pages to print on the General tab first.

    Choosing the binding#

    • Saddle stitch (the default): every sheet is folded inside the one before and the booklet is stapled through the fold. For eight pages, the outer sheet holds pages 8 and 1 on one side and 2 and 7 on the other; the inner sheet holds 6 and 3, then 4 and 5. Best for thin booklets.
    • Perfect bound: the sheets go in signatures, small groups of Sheets per signature (4 at first, 1 to 32) folded together. The signatures are stacked and glued at the spine. The last signature takes the sheets left over. With one sheet per signature the pages pair as 4 1, 2 3, then 8 5, 6 7. Best for thicker booklets.

    The settings#

    • Sheets per signature (perfect bound only): how many sheets are folded together in each signature.
    • Creep (saddle stitch only, 0 to 20 mm, 0 at first): when nested sheets are folded, the inner ones stick out at the open edge and lose more when the booklet is trimmed. Creep moves the pages of the innermost sheet this far toward the spine; the sheets between move in proportion and the outer sheet does not move. The crop marks move with the pages.
    • Gap (0 to 100 mm, 0 at first): the space between the two pages of a spread, centred on the fold. On a sheet sized to the pages, the sheet grows by the gap.

    Blank pages and the counts#

    A booklet always has a multiple of four pages, since each sheet holds four. When the drawing has another number, blank pages are added at the back. Under the sketch, the summary gives the number of pages, the blank pages added and the sheets of paper printed on both sides, and for a perfect-bound booklet the number of signatures.

    With Crop marks on the Prepress tab, a booklet also gets a dashed fold mark at the spine. See Printing a drawing for the other marks.

    Tip: Print one copy first and fold it to check the order and the margins before you print them all.
