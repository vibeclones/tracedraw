    Drawing with a graphics tablet

    A graphics tablet's pen moves the pointer and clicks like a mouse, so every tool works with it. Where the system also tells TraceDraw how hard the pen presses, the Freehand and Brush Strokes tools let that pressure set the width of the stroke: press lightly for a thin line, harder for a wide one. The mouse draws an even width, as before.

    Drawing with pressure#

    1. Choose the Freehand tool, or the Brush Strokes tool in the Preset, Brush or Expression mode.
    2. Draw with the pen, pressing harder where the stroke should be wider.
    • Freehand: the new line gets a variable-width outline that follows the pressure along it. A feather-light touch gives a hairline, full pressure the full outline width of new lines. You can edit the line with the Shape tool and its outline in the Properties panel, as any line with a variable width (see Outlines).
    • Brush Strokes: the pen scales the width of Preset, Brush and Expression strokes. The Pressure setting of the Brush Strokes panel (0.5 at first) says how much the width follows the pen: at 0.5 a stroke with no pressure is half as wide as at full pressure; at 1 it goes from nothing to the full width. Preset and Brush keep their shape along the stroke (a taper, a wave) and scale it by the pen; Expression takes the pen's pressure in place of the drawing speed. Calligraphic strokes and the sprayer's objects keep their size.

    The stroke shows as a thin line while you draw and takes its widths when you lift the pen. Apply to a curve in the Brush Strokes panel has no pen, so it builds the stroke without pressure.

    Turning pressure off#

    The pressure is on at first. To draw with the pen at an even width, turn off Pen pressure sets the width of Freehand and Brush Strokes drags under Graphics tablet on the Freehand/Bézier page of Tools > Options > Tools.

    Which systems give the pressure#

    SystemThe pen moves the pointerPressure
    Windows 8 and lateryesyes
    Linux with X11yesyes
    Linux with Waylandno (see below)no

    On a Wayland desktop (the default of several Linux systems) the pen does not reach TraceDraw yet. Start TraceDraw with tracedraw --x11 from a terminal: the window then opens through XWayland, where the pen moves the pointer, clicks and reports its pressure.

    Tip: For more control over the stroke afterwards, draw with pressure, then adjust the width nodes with the Variable Outline tool.
